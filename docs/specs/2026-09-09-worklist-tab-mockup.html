Worklist, without touching the parts that work

The row is not the problem. Managing worklists is, and so is the chrome around them: this is the last panel still built from inline styles, browser prompts and text buttons. One switcher line fixes the panel, and the catalog that already exists becomes the place where all worklists live.

  • The cyan selection band stays where it is. Not touched here at all. Worth its own look later, on its own terms.

What is actually inconsistent

  1. It cannot follow a theme. The panel is styled entirely inline, against variable names that do not exist in the stylesheet, so it runs on hardcoded dark values while every other panel recolours.
  2. Creating a worklist opens a browser prompt. Wiping and deleting open browser confirms. Renaming, on the other hand, is already inline and is the model the other two should follow.
  3. The switcher is a wrapping row of pills with New and Import mixed into the same row. Three tickets take two lines, and a busy bench takes four.
  4. Copy, Wipe and a red cross are text buttons in a 300 pixel header, where the app now uses quiet icon buttons and a menu. Highlight is a different case: it is a feature and should read like one.
  5. Typed glyphs where the app uses icons. A literal ✕ for remove and delete, ▸ ▾ for the two folds.
  6. The Worklists catalog cannot open anything. It lists every worklist on the device as plain text.

The panel: one switcher line

The wrapping pill block becomes a single line naming the open worklist. Clicking it drops down the rest, with New, Paste and a way through to the full catalog at the foot. Nothing wraps, however many tickets a board collects.

The catalog becomes the index

There is no need for a second index inside the panel, because one already exists: Tools ▸ Workbench ▸ Worklists, which lists every worklist stored on this device. Today it is printed text that cannot be opened. Give its rows the counts, the progress and a click, and it is the index, reachable from the switcher line above and from the rail.

Worth saying plainly: this lives under Tools, not in the Library panel. Library's tabs are history, metadata, folders, board number and PDF. If you would rather it sat in the Library, that is a small move and a separate decision — the link works the same either way.

Highlighting, kept as a feature

Lighting the worklist on the board is the reason to keep the list open while working, so it does not become an anonymous icon in a row of icons. It stays a named control in the header, lit in the accent when on, and it keeps its current behaviour: outlines in each entry's mark colour, plus the glow on nets shared by more than one entry.

One thing I am not deciding for you

The three reading slots on a net row. They render whether or not they hold a value, so an untouched net is two lines. That is either a waste of a narrow panel, or it is the point: three labelled boxes waiting to be filled in are a prompt to measure, and a bench probably wants them there.

The design above keeps them exactly as they are today. If you want them to appear only once a reading exists, that is a one-line change and I will show it, but it is your call, not a cleanup.

Settled: the switcher line in the panel, and the existing catalog as the index behind it. That is one new line of chrome, one menu, and a list that already exists finally doing something when you click it.
