A · Ribbon button (recommended). One more button in the ribbon, after the diode button; it costs no board space until opened. The popover lists each rule with its colour, its keywords and how many pins on this board it colours. Hovering a row would light just those pins; clicking would dim every other pin until clicked again. That turns the legend into a filter: show me every I²C pin on this board.

B · Strip on the board. Always visible, closable, one line of short names. It is the fork's approach. It answers "what is pink" at a glance, but it sits over the board permanently, it cannot show keywords or counts, and it would compete with the minimap and the slide-mode guide in the same corner.

C · Sidebar Layers tab. A "Pin colours" section under the layers, with the same rows as A. It needs the sidebar open, which covers part of the board, but it is where the other "what is drawn and how" controls already live. It could also be added alongside A.

Every placement shows the same thing. The green "no rule matched" colour is listed because it is the most common pin colour on most boards. Its bottom-side twin is red, the same red family as the power rails, and nothing on screen says so today.
